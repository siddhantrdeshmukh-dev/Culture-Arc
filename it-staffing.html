Our Methodology

4-Stage Precision Screening

Every candidate goes through a rigorous, multi-dimensional evaluation designed to validate both technical excellence and cultural alignment before a single profile reaches your desk.

01

Technical Skills Assessment

Role-specific coding challenges, system design reviews, and domain knowledge tests curated by senior engineers.

02

Cultural & Behavioural Fit

Structured competency interviews and psychometric evaluations to assess leadership, collaboration, and adaptability.

03

Background Verification (10 Steps)

Comprehensive 10-point checks including employment records, education, criminal history, credit, and drug testing.

04

Client Match & Deployment

Precise profile-to-role matching followed by onboarding support and 90-day performance tracking.
