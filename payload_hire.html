Operational Details.

Clarity is the foundation of execution. We utilize deterministic Service Level Agreements guaranteeing speed, quality, and strict regulatory adherence.

What is the standard time-to-fill for specialized technical roles?

Our proprietary AI parsing engine mixed with our active bench allows us to submit the first batch of heavily vetted candidates within 48 to 72 hours for standard roles (Full Stack, QA, Cloud Ops). Executive search mandates typically resolve within 3-5 weeks depending on the equity parameters.

How does CultureArc handle compliance across multiple jurisdictions?

We operate as the Employer of Record (EOR) in regions where we provide staffing. We absorb all liabilities related to co-employment, taxation, local labor law compliance, and statutory benefits, providing you with a clean, low-risk invoice.

Do you provide Contract-to-Hire capabilities?

Yes. Our Contract-to-Hire model is highly utilized. Clients typically evaluate our contractors over a 6 to 12-month period before converting them to their internal payroll, entirely bridging the risk of a bad hire.
